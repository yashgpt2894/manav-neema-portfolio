# Page Topology

1. **Fixed navigation**
   - Desktop: wordmark, section links, LinkedIn, resume CTA
   - Mobile: compact wordmark, resume CTA, menu toggle and full-screen menu
2. **Hero**
   - Oversized `MANAV` wordmark behind the portrait
   - Portrait-led composition with glass capability and experience chips
   - Role statement, availability note, and two CTAs
3. **Journey**
   - Introductory editorial heading
   - 2021-2026 narrative timeline
   - Scroll reveal for each milestone
4. **Selected impact**
   - Dark contrast section
   - Three case-study cards derived from resume achievements
5. **Capability overview**
   - Skills grouped into Data Platforms, ML Systems, and Delivery
   - Scrolling technology marquee
6. **Education and proof**
   - Joint UvA/VU M.Sc., NMIMS B.Tech, teaching assistant role, certification
7. **Contact**
   - Large closing statement and direct email / LinkedIn / resume routes
8. **Footer**
   - Location, role focus, copyright, back-to-top

The page is a single document with anchored sections. Navigation is fixed above the page. No backend, authentication, or form submission is required.
